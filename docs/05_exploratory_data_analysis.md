# Exploratory Data Analysis

## Purpose

The exploratory data analysis (EDA) was used to understand the cleaned NYC Yellow Taxi dataset and identify patterns in demand, revenue, trip characteristics, and operational performance.

The analysis was implemented in `src/05_exploratory_data_analysis.py` and helped guide the SQL analysis and Power BI dashboard.

---

## Business Questions

The EDA focused on four main areas.

### Demand

- When are taxi trips most frequent?
- How does demand vary by hour of the day?
- How does demand differ across weekdays?

### Revenue

- During which hours is the highest revenue generated?
- How does revenue vary throughout the day?
- What are the tipping patterns?

### Trip Characteristics

- What is the distribution of trip duration?
- What is the distribution of trip distance?
- How are trip distance and duration related?

### Operational Performance

- What is the average trip speed?
- How does average speed vary throughout the day?
- How does trip efficiency change across different times of day?
